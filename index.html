Fraud-Spike Sentinel

August 2026

An explainable fraud-detection pipeline for payment merchants — built to flag risk, not to auto-block real customers.

  • Combines a rolling z-score rule with an Isolation Forest over 11 engineered features to catch card-testing bursts, takeovers, geo-velocity spikes, and refund-abuse rings.
  • Bounded, human-in-the-loop rules engine: Monitor → Step-Up Auth → Soft Hold → Escalate Human, with a hard rupee ceiling on autonomous action.
  • Claude-powered plain-English narration of flags, adaptive self-tuning thresholds, and an immutable audit trail.
  • Reports honest precision/recall/F1/PR-AUC on a time-based split across 3 merchants, with a live REST scoring endpoint and demo dashboard.
